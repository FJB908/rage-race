# Gems, premium cosmetics and the streak calendar

**Gems** are the premium currency (`rr_gems` in localStorage, helpers `gemCount()`, `addGems(n)`).

How a player gets gems:
- **Real money:** the Gems tab in the shop (`src/data/gems.js`). Buying is a stub until billing exists: set `window.GEM_STORE = { buy(pack, grant) { ... grant(pack.gems) } }` and the buy buttons use it.
- **Daily streak:** gems on days 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 29 (170 in total). See `src/data/streak.js`.
- **Luck:** an epic drop has a 1.2% chance of 5 gems, a legendary drop 5% for 5 to 20 gems (`resolveDrop` in `game.js`).

**Premium cosmetics** live in `src/data/premium-cosmetics.js` (generated by `tools/gen_premium.py`). They cost gems (`gemPrice`), never coins, are not in normal drops, and can only drop from a legendary drop with a 0.4% chance. `exclusive:true` items (the streak's day-30 skin) are never sold.

Premium art can animate: layers accept `spin`, `orbit` (+`pivot`), `bob`, `pulse` and `rainbow` (see `drawCustomLayers` in `game.js`).

**Streak calendar:** `src/ui/streak.js`. One claim per local day; missing a day restarts at day 1; day 30 gives the exclusive skin and then the cycle restarts. `STREAK_CONFIG.autoOpen` shows it once per launch when a reward is waiting.
