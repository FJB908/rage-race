# Powerup ideas (to approve)

## Replacements for Wind (the Stun bomb is built, see POWERUPS.md; the rest is still a proposal)
Why Wind fails: it pushes every player the same way, so they all end up on the same edge. These hit one player or one spot instead, and never push sideways. Wind's share of the rolls would go to whichever of these you pick.

1. **Freeze ray**: the platform of the player directly ahead of you turns to ice for 5 s (their next landings slide). One target, readable, no clumping.
2. **Boomerang**: thrown up the track, knocks the first player ahead off their platform and flies back to you. Needs a clear shot; misses if nobody is in the lane.
3. **Bomb trap**: a bomb lands on the leader's platform and goes off after 2 s with a visible warning ring; anyone on that platform is thrown off. Players can jump away in time.
4. **Slime puddle**: you leave a puddle on your own platform; the next player who lands there slides off. A trap that waits, one victim.
5. **Sticky gum**: the player directly ahead gets 40% less jump power for 5 s.
6. **Anchor**: the player directly ahead falls 50% faster for 5 s (shorter, heavier jumps).
7. **Swap**: swap places with the player directly ahead (only for 3rd and 4th). Dramatic comeback, rare.
8. **Decoy box**: a fake item box on a platform ahead; whoever grabs it gets nothing and a sad sound. Harmless and funny.

My three: Freeze ray, Boomerang and Bomb trap (all distinct from each other and from the quake).

"Who" = who rolls it most (the game gives comeback tools to players further back, defence to players in front).

## Comeback (further back)
1. **Magnet**: for 6 s your jump aims itself at the nearest platform ahead. Great for beginners. Who: back half.
2. **Cannon**: a cannon appears on your platform; tap to launch about 600 px straight up the track. Who: last two.
3. **Freeze ray**: the leader's platform turns to ice for 5 s (slippery landing). Who: back half.
4. **Slime puddle**: leave a puddle on your platform; whoever lands there next slides off. A trap that waits. Who: anyone.
5. **Boomerang**: thrown up the track, hits the first player ahead (knocks them off their platform) and flies back. Who: back half.
6. **Sticky gum**: the player directly ahead gets 40% less jump power for 5 s. Who: 3rd/4th.
7. **Jester hat**: the nearest player ahead gets reversed aim for 4 s (funny, not cruel). Who: 2nd-4th.
8. **Swap**: swap places with the player directly ahead of you (only when you are 3rd or 4th). Who: back only.

## Boosts (for yourself)
9. **Spring boots**: your next 3 jumps reach 30% further.
10. **Double jump**: one extra tap in mid-air. Simple, skill friendly.
11. **Moon jump**: low gravity for 5 s, longer and floatier jumps.
12. **Focus**: slow motion for 5 s while you aim, with a longer aim line. A good beginner tool.
13. **Bridge**: two temporary platforms appear ahead of you for 6 s.
14. **Portal**: place an entry portal; the exit appears about 500 px higher.

## Defence (for the leader)
15. **Mirror shield**: like Shield, but the next attack bounces back to whoever sent it.
16. **Safety net**: if you fall in the next 8 s you land back on your last platform at once, with no time loss.
17. **Ghost**: for 3 s you pass through ceilings and spikes.

## Fun and meta
18. **Coin rain**: a burst of bonus coins at the end of the race (shown with a small shower).
19. **Confetti bomb**: harmless fun, all players get a short party effect (a sound-and-emote moment).

## My top picks
Double jump, Magnet, Safety net, Freeze ray and Boomerang: they are easy to read, each has a clear use, and they fit the "comeback for the back, defence for the front" rule.
