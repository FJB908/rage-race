RAGE RACE - Privacy policy

Last updated:

This policy explains what data the game RAGE RACE ("the game") uses and why. Contact: [YOUR E-MAIL ADDRESS].

What we store

  • Game progress: coins, gems, items you own, levels, statistics, settings. It is stored on your device and, so that it is not lost, in our cloud database (Google Firebase).
  • Account: the game signs you in anonymously. If you choose "Sign in with Google", we receive your Google account ID and e-mail address, only to link your save to you.
  • Public profile: your player name, your look (skin, hat, ...), your level and rank. Other players can see this, for example when you add friends or join a party.
  • Friends and parties: friend requests, party invites and live party race positions, while you use these features.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads. In the EU/UK you are asked for consent first. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Purchases

Gem purchases go through Google Play. We never see your payment details; Google Play tells us only that a purchase was made.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for anything other than running the game, showing ads and keeping your save safe. The game is not directed at children under 13.

Delete your data

In the game: Settings > Delete my account and data. This permanently removes your account, your cloud save, your public profile and your friendships, and clears the data on your device. You can also e-mail us and we will delete your data.

Third parties

Google Firebase (storage and sign-in), Google AdMob (ads), Google Play (purchases). Their own privacy policies apply to the data they handle.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page.
